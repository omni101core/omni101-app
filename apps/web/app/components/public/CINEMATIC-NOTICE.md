# Cinematic experience — third-party attribution

Adapted portions: cylinder position/UV/index construction and cylinder texture/darkness vertex/fragment shaders from **Joseph Santamaria / Codrops**, Cinematic 3D Scroll Experiences with GSAP (2025), variant-1 `src/lib/variant-1/utils.ts` and `shaders.ts`. Camera choreography follows the variant-2 scroll-driven camera/target approach. Adaptations: Three.js BufferGeometry/ShaderMaterial, original photographic atlas and software textures, demand rendering, opacity, accessible DOM chapters and OMNI101 camera paths.
